Safety & Community Guidelines

Kindred is designed to support friendly, respectful social connection. Online matching can never remove all risk, so everyone should use their own judgment.

For first meetings

  • Meet in a busy public place.
  • Tell someone you trust where you are going.
  • Arrange your own transport and keep control of when you leave.
  • Do not share sensitive personal or financial information with someone you have just met.
  • Leave immediately if a situation feels unsafe.

Community standards

Harassment, threats, hate, sexual exploitation, scams, impersonation and attempts to obtain private information are not welcome. Content that encourages illegal or dangerous activity may be removed.

Reporting

As community features are introduced, reporting and blocking controls should be available where people interact. Reports may be reviewed to protect the community and enforce these guidelines.

Emergencies

Kindred is not an emergency service. If you or another person is in immediate danger, contact the appropriate local emergency service.
