Talk to the people behind Kindred.

Questions, feedback and safety reports help us build a more useful community.

General feedback

You can contact the project owner through the public GitHub profile. Please do not publish private addresses, identity documents or other sensitive information in a public issue.

Safety and content reports

If content appears threatening, hateful, sexually explicit, fraudulent or illegal, use the in-product report option where available. For urgent danger, contact local emergency services rather than waiting for an online response.

Account and privacy requests

Requests concerning account data, correction or deletion should identify the relevant Kindred account without including passwords or authentication codes. We may ask for reasonable verification before acting on an account request.
