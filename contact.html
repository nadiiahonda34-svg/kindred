Contact Kindred

Questions, feedback, safety concerns and partnership enquiries are welcome.

Kindred is currently an evolving project. A dedicated public support email will be published here before the service begins accepting persistent user accounts or public community submissions.

Safety concerns

If your message concerns harmful community content or behavior, please include enough context to identify the issue, but do not send passwords, payment details, identity documents or other highly sensitive information.

Website feedback

You can also use the project's public GitHub repository to review the website's development while Kindred is in its prototype stage.
