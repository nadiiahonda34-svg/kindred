About Kindred

Kindred is being built for people who want meaningful local friendships without the pressure of a dating app or a huge social network.

The idea is simple: shared interests, compatible social energy and a concrete plan can make meeting new people easier. Kindred focuses on small groups, useful conversation prompts and public first meetups.

What we are building

Kindred combines interest-based discovery, small social circles, community stories and practical guides for people who are new to a city, rebuilding a social life or simply looking for more genuine connections.

Our principles

We design for privacy, user control, respectful participation and low-pressure connection. Exact locations should not be exposed to strangers, and people should always be able to leave or report an interaction.

Editorial approach

Our guides are written to be practical, specific and useful. We aim to publish original material about friendship, belonging, conversation, relocation and safer in-person socializing.

Kindred is currently an evolving project. Features shown on the site may change as the service develops.
