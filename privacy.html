Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 August 2026.

This page explains the privacy approach for the current Kindred website. The service is still being developed, and this policy will be updated before features that collect or persist additional personal data are launched.

Information you enter

The website may present fields for a city, interests, social preferences, stories or discussion topics. Prototype interactions should not be treated as private storage. Do not enter sensitive personal, financial, medical or identification information.

Technical data

Our hosting provider may process ordinary technical information required to deliver the website, such as IP address, browser information and request logs, according to its own policies.

Advertising and cookies

Kindred may in the future use advertising services such as Google AdSense. If advertising, analytics or other technologies requiring consent are introduced, this policy and the site's consent controls will be updated before those technologies are enabled where required.

Third-party services

Features such as authentication or hosting may rely on third-party providers. Their processing practices are governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

Your choices

Do not submit information you do not want processed. When persistent accounts and community publishing are enabled, Kindred intends to provide appropriate controls for account data and published content.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the method listed on our Contact page.
